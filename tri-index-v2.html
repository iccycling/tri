Student-run, all levels welcome

ICTRI is a student-led community at Imperial College London, backed by the Student Union. Whether you're chasing a podium or your first triathlon, there's a session for you — most members start with no multisport experience at all.

We train together across all three disciplines, race together at events across the UK, and look out for each other along the way.
